How to Delete Your DineEase Account

Delete Account In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the DineEase app
  2. Go to Profile / Account Settings
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • Your booking history
  • Your saved preferences

Data Retention

All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
